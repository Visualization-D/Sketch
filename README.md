Creative Sketch Pro — Version 9 Release Notes
Version 9 expands creative control with advanced brush simulations, reusable image textures, customizable paper surfaces, and a more flexible editing workflow.
Brushes and painting

    Create full-color, repeating texture brushes from a canvas selection or imported image.
    Use the new Mixer Brush to blend selected paint with colors already on the canvas. Paint load, mixing, and wetness are adjustable.
    Enhance watercolor painting with configurable water, bleed, edge darkening, granulation, and drying.
    Add natural variation using size, opacity, angle, and scatter dynamics.
    Adjust texture scale and flip brush tips horizontally or vertically.

Canvas and drawing guides

    Choose from A4, square, Full HD, 4K, and vertical-story canvas presets.
    Work on colored or transparent surfaces.
    Add watercolor paper, artist canvas, linen, or recycled-paper textures.
    Display square, isometric, circular, or dot grids with adjustable size, color, and opacity.
    Paper textures and grids remain non-destructive and do not alter layer pixels or transparent PNG exports.

Editing and workflow

    Drag images directly onto the canvas or layer list to create new layers.
    Use full-screen mode with standard editing shortcuts, including undo, redo, cut, copy, paste, select all, duplicate, and save.
    Open the built-in shortcut guide with ?.
    Keep up to 300 history steps, with older steps recycled automatically and without interruption.
    Enjoy more reliable undo and redo across strokes, layer changes, and canvas settings.

Export and compatibility

    Removed OpenRaster (ORA) export.
    Retained transparent PNG and layered PSD export.
    Updated application branding and export metadata to Creative Sketch Pro V9.
